# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume good faith. Critique the work, not the person.

## What this means here

Most friction in this project will be about whether something is a good skill.
That is a judgement call, and reasonable people will disagree. When you disagree:

- Argue about the skill, not the contributor.
- Say what would change your mind.
- Remember that "this isn't a good fit" is not a verdict on anyone's competence.

## Not acceptable

Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, or unwelcome attention of
any kind. Publishing others' private information. Sustained disruption of
discussions.

## Scope

Applies in issues, pull requests, discussions, and anywhere someone is
representing the project.

## Enforcement

Report to the maintainer via a GitHub issue or a direct message. Reports are
handled confidentially. Consequences range from a warning to a permanent ban,
proportionate to what happened.
